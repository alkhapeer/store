قد تحتوي ألعابنا على روابط خارجية لخدمات أخرى (مثل واتساب، تيليجرام، متاجر التطبيقات). عند الضغط عليها تنطبق سياسات خصوصية تلك الخدمات، ولا نتحمل مسؤوليتها.

Last updated: October 16, 2026 · Version 1.0
🔒 Summary: HeroGames does not collect any personal data. All progress is stored locally on your device only. No accounts, no sign-up, no tracking.

1. Scope

This policy applies to all games and web apps published by HeroGames, including but not limited to Open Topic (a bilingual question card game) and any future titles.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit: name, email, phone, location, contacts, photos, device ID, browsing history, payment data, or advertising identifiers. There are no user accounts and no sign-up.

3. Local Storage

Some preferences and game progress (levels, scores, achievements, language preference) are saved via localStorage on your device only. Clearing browser data will erase them permanently, and we hold no copy.

4. Third-Party Services

Some games use limited external services without any personal tracking:

  • Google Fonts — to load Arabic and Latin typefaces. Google may log your IP address per Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive this data.
  • External QR generation service (in some games) — only used to render a QR code for a URL, containing no personal data.

Some games may link to external services (WhatsApp, Telegram, app stores). Their privacy policies apply when you visit them.

5. Permissions

We do not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, SMS, external storage, or notifications. Only optional permissions (Web Audio, Vibration, Web Share) plus mandatory Local Storage are used.

6. Children's Privacy

Suitable for ages 12+. Some titles include a family-friendly mode suitable for all ages. Because we collect no data, our games are compliant with Saudi PDPL, EU GDPR, and US COPPA.

7. Data Security

All data is stored locally on your device. No external databases, no accounts, no cloud sync. Our principle: you cannot breach what we don't hold.

8. Your Rights

You can access, delete, or export your data at any time by clearing browser storage, or by disabling localStorage (the game will still run, but progress won't be saved).

9. Changes

We may update this policy as we add features or new games. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects any changes.
